Campfire Evenings

As the Ooty evening chill sets in, gather around the campfire under the stars. Share stories, listen to music, and let The Groot wrap you in warmth. A signature Groot experience.

Home-Cooked Meals

Authentic, home-cooked Tamil meals prepared with care and local ingredients. The kind of food that makes you feel at home, far from home. Available on request.

Step out to cool mist, crisp air and the sounds of the forest waking up. Morning walks, filter coffee and the magic golden light through the trees — Ooty mornings are unforgettable.
