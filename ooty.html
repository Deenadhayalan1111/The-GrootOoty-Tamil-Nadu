Explore Ooty

From The Groot, all of Ooty's wonders are within reach.

The Queen of the Nilgiris

Ooty (Udhagamandalam) sits at 2,240 metres in the Nilgiri hills of Tamil Nadu. Known for its rolling tea estates, eucalyptus forests, colonial-era bungalows and cool climate � it has been India's most beloved hill station for over a century. The Groot places you right at the heart of it.

Garden

Government Botanical Garden

A stunning 55-acre garden established in 1848, showcasing an extraordinary diversity of rare plants, century-old trees and colourful floral displays. The annual Ooty Flower Show takes place here. A must-visit.

~5 km from The Groot Half day
Lake

Ooty Lake

A scenic artificial lake built in 1825, perfect for boating, cycling along the banks and peaceful walks with panoramic Nilgiri hill views. A calm, beautiful spot in the heart of Ooty town.

~6 km from The Groot 2�3 hours
Nature

Nilgiri Tea Estates

Walk through rolling hills covered in emerald green tea bushes. The Nilgiri tea is world-famous for its light, fragrant flavour. Visit a tea factory, see how it's processed, and taste it fresh from the source.

Nearby & Coonoor Half to full day
Trek

Doddabetta Peak

At 2,637 metres, Doddabetta is the highest point in the Nilgiris. On a clear day, the views stretch for kilometres over the rolling hills. There's also an observatory with a telescope for stellar views.

~9 km from Ooty Half day
