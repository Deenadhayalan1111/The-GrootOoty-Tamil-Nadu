Explore Ooty

From The Groot, all of Ooty's wonders are within reach.

The Queen of the Nilgiris

Ooty (Udhagamandalam) sits at 2,240 metres in the Nilgiri hills of Tamil Nadu. Known for its rolling tea estates, eucalyptus forests, colonial-era bungalows and cool climate — it has been India's most beloved hill station for over a century. The Groot places you right at the heart of it.
